What Warden will not do

  • It never pretends to be you. It trades through the agent wallet you paired in the Binance app, and every receipt says Warden placed the order.
  • It never suggests a rule or a stock. You write the rule.
  • It cannot raise your wallet limits. Those are set in the Binance app.
  • It does not trade when the issuer restricts the token for earnings. It records the refusal instead.
  • It does not promise a price. If the quote is more than 1.5% from the market price, it stops.
