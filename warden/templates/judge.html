How to check this yourself

Warden executes a holder's own rule around an earnings print through Binance Agentic Wallet, after checking the issuer's live status, the wallet session and lock, the balance, the quote and the daily quota. Every run leaves a receipt with a hash; executed ones carry a BscScan transaction.
